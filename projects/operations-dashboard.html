ServiceNow Analytics Product

A full-stack analytics product I built and shipped end to end at IQVIA — centralizing Incident, Request, and Change ticket status and queue metrics into a single leadership view so decisions happen with live data instead of Monday-morning spreadsheets.

RoleEnd-to-end owner & developer
OrganizationIQVIA R&DS
TypeFull-stack analytics product

The problem

Managers had to piece together operational status from ServiceNow reports, spreadsheets, and ad-hoc chat threads. By the time the picture was complete, it was already out of date — and ageing tickets in the wrong queue could go unnoticed until they became incidents in their own right.

The build

  • Modelled the data in Snowflake, pulling ticket state from ServiceNow so historical trends and current queue depths lived in one place.
  • Wrote a FastAPI backend that served aggregated metrics on demand and handled auth against the internal identity provider.
  • Built the analytics UI in Python/Dash with Plotly visualisations and Pandas transformations — filters for team, priority, and age drop straight into the same query layer.
  • Packaged and shipped the app as a Docker container so it could be deployed and updated repeatably.
  • Used MS Copilot during development to accelerate boilerplate and stay focused on the product decisions that mattered.

Impact

  • Gave leadership real-time visibility into Incident, Request, and Change queues — no more waiting on manual round-ups.
  • Accelerated management decision-making with a single trusted source of operational metrics.
  • Reduced the ambient reporting overhead that used to consume team-lead time each week.

What I learned

  • Owning something end to end forces you to think in product terms even for internal tools — the "so what?" question sharpens every widget.
  • Shipping to production changes what you value: observability, error handling, and rollback matter more than a clever chart.
  • The right stack for a small analytics product isn't the fanciest one — it's the one you can build, ship, and support alone.
